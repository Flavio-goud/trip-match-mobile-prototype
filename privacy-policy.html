Trip Match Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Trip Match is a travel companion and local activity matching application.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features used, Trip Match may collect account information, profile details, messages, trip or activity content, and identity verification information submitted by users.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide the service, match users, improve safety and trust features, support identity verification, facilitate messaging and group coordination, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that support app operations, security, hosting, communications, or verification workflows, when necessary to provide the service or comply with the law.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect user information.

User choices

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their account data by contacting us.
